## 🕰️ සිංහල වේලාව — HelaClock Wall v1.2

A living Sigiriya live wallpaper that tells the time in spoken Sinhala. Free, with no ads and no tracking.

### 📥 Install
1. Download **`HelaClock-Wall-v1.2.apk`** below.
2. Open the downloaded file on your phone. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.
3. Open **සිංහල වේලාව** and tap **Set as wallpaper**.

Requires **Android 7.0 or newer**.
