**Sinhala clock live wallpaper.** Shows the time in spoken Sinhala over a living scene of Sigiriya that changes through dawn, morning, noon, evening and night — mist, swaying trees, drifting clouds, random midday rain, storms with lightning, rainbows, real moon phases, Poya lamps, Vesak lanterns, butterflies, egrets, a peacock, monkeys, lotus and water lilies, a diving kingfisher, jumping fish, parrot flocks, a soaring kite, falling leaves and an elephant herd at dusk. Optional live weather for 13 Sri Lankan cities. No ads, no tracking, free.
