# Privacy Policy — සිංහල වේලාව (Sinhala Welawa Live Wallpaper)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

**සිංහලෙන් කෙටියෙන්:** මේ app එක ඔබගේ කිසිම පුද්ගලික තොරතුරක් එකතු කරන්නේ නැහැ, යවන්නේ නැහැ, ගබඩා කරන්නේ නැහැ. දැන්වීම් සහ tracking නැහැ.

## What we collect
Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

## Network use
The app works fully offline. Only if you turn on **"Live weather"** in settings, it requests the current weather from
[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) about every 30 minutes. The request contains only the latitude and longitude of the
Sri Lankan city you picked in settings (for example Sigiriya). It contains no personal data, no location from your device
and no device identifiers. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to that request.

## Permissions
- `INTERNET` — only used for the optional live weather described above.
- The device tilt sensor is read only while the wallpaper is visible, to create a parallax effect. Nothing is stored or sent.

## Settings
Your settings (font, style, options) are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

## Children
The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Contact
For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
